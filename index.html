The Philosophy

The All-Terrain AI

Like OpenClaw, standard AI agents get root access. They do everything, but require constant handholding to get the context right. They generate noise.

  • Cloud-dependent endpoints
  • Unpredictable outputs
  • Loss of personal voice

The City Car AI

SlowClaw is sandboxed. It takes your raw thoughts (notes, audio, video) and carefully curates them into thoughtful workflows you approve.

  • Strict local sandbox
  • Social UI (Like to Publish)
  • Your Voice. Your Pace.

Built for the Mindful Creator

🔒

Sandboxed & Secure

Your data stays on your device. Workflows are isolated scripts. Nothing gets published without your explicit consent via our Social UI.

📱

Mobile-First Hub

Your phone is the always-on data server. Sync with your laptop for heavy lifting, but review and approve content seamlessly on the go.

🧩

Community Skills

Like ZeroClaw, performance matters. Share and download local Rust-powered workflows. Let the community build the tools, you provide the ideas.
