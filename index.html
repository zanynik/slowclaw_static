Private Journaling. Durable Output. Human Curation.

Turn your journals into posts, clips, plans, and a feed that actually feels like yours.

slowclaw.social is a local-first journaling and curation product. You dump thoughts in text, audio, or video. SlowClaw turns that raw material into durable artifacts you can keep, edit, publish, or use privately: short posts, blog drafts, article drafts, clip plans, todos, and events.

Phase 01: Capture

Journal first. Structure later.

Multimodal Logging

Whether it's an audio brain dump, a quick video diary, or a long-form text rant. Capture your raw, unfiltered thoughts exactly as they arrive.

100% Private

Everything is stored firmly on your local workspace. No cloud syncs, no data scraping. Your thoughts are yours alone, powered by local intelligence.

Phase 02: Output

Generate artifacts you can keep, not just AI summaries you forget.

The core product output is durable artifacts: files under posts/, clip plans, todos, events, and long-form drafts. AI helps package your raw material into formats you can actually use.

Publishing Outputs

Turn journals into short posts, blog drafts, article drafts, ebook seeds, private essays, and transcript-aware clip plans. Edit them if you want. Keep them if they matter. Publish only what represents you.

  • Artifact-first generation instead of abstraction-first storage
  • Short posts can be tweet-style or simple personal insights
  • Articles and private writing live beside public drafts

Planner Outputs

When your journals contain commitments, follow-ups, or scheduling information, SlowClaw writes todos and events into the app so the product is useful even when you are not trying to publish anything.

Phase 03: Curation

Curate from what you keep, not only from what you post.

Kept Artifacts Become Signals

What stays in posts/, what you refine into articles, and what clip plans survive are strong signals of what you actually care about, even if you never publish publicly.

Open Social Inputs

Bring in sources like Bluesky and Nostr, then rank them against your private artifact history and journal-derived interests instead of raw engagement farming.

Transcript Editing Superpower

Segments and timestamps let AI point to the interesting parts while deterministic tools do the actual cutting. That makes long recordings easier to turn into clean, watchable clips.

Phase 04: Get Started

Quick Start

1. Prerequisites

Required: Rust toolchain (rustc, cargo)

Optional: Node.js 18+ if you need to rebuild web assets

2. Build

cargo build --release

Binary path: ./target/release/slowclaw

3. Run

./target/release/slowclaw gateway

Or for scheduling + automation:

./target/release/slowclaw daemon

4. Product Direction

SlowClaw is not trying to be generic AI slop generation. The product loop is:

  • capture privately
  • generate durable artifacts
  • keep or publish selectively
  • curate the internet from what matters to you
